How the verbal duel works

Both players draft from the same nine phrase cards. Every useful phrase you take is also a phrase your rival cannot use.

Build valid English

Start with a noun. Complete it with either an adjective clause, or a verb plus another noun.

Noun+Adjective
Noun+Verb+Noun

Steal the vocabulary

The middle board is shared. Drafting a vital noun or conjunction can strand the other player with an unfinished sentence.

Combos and weak spots

Repeat the exact same noun in consecutive completed roasts to create a rising combo. Use phrases tagged to the rival's weak spot for a critical multiplier.

Continuations

Use an ellipsis to carry your unfinished line into the next round. A rival roast worth 16 or more breaks your concentration and erases the carried line.

Comebacks

Taking damage charges three comeback bars. At 20, 40, or 60 charge, end a complete sentence with a character-specific closure worth 4, 10, or 18 extra damage.

Grammar sabotage

Invalid cards stay clickable. Choosing one removes it from play, costs 3 Pride, and ends your sentence with zero damage. Sometimes terrible grammar is a tactical crime.
